Give

Research is the expensive part, and it is the part that keeps this honest.

Anyone can write a reentry pamphlet once. Keeping it accurate as scams, platforms, financial products and hiring practice all change underneath you is the work, and it is the work that money buys.

We publish what we can prove. No impact numbers on this page until they have been measured.

Five things, and none of them is a gala.

01

Keeping it current

Ongoing research so the lessons describe the world as it is this year, not the year the program was written.

02

One to one time

Sitting down with people coming home and building a kit around their actual situation. This does not scale cheaply and it is the whole product.

03

Collecting stories

Reaching people who have been released, handling consent properly, and turning what they learned into curriculum.

04

Art supplies and facilitation

The Better Neighbors foundation runs on real materials in real rooms. Pencils and paper inside a facility, and a facilitator standing in front of people.

05

Paying our own people

An organisation built on lived experience that expects formerly incarcerated people to work for free is not a model. It is the same problem in a new shirt.

Talk to us

We are not publishing a cost per person served.

Every nonprofit page has a line like "$25 provides a starter kit for one person". Ours will say that when we have measured it and can show the working. Until then it would be a number invented to make giving feel good, which is exactly the kind of thing this organisation exists to teach people to spot.

Online giving is not switched on yet.

The membership levels above are real and they are what we are building toward. The payment side is not live, and we are not going to put a button here that takes a card into a process that is not finished.

If you want to back this now, write to us and we will tell you exactly where the organisation stands, what is confirmed and what is not, and how a gift would be handled. You will get a straight answer even if the straight answer is wait a few weeks.
