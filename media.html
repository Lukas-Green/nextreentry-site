Media

Other people have been telling this story for years.

Television, radio, podcasts, press and galleries. Every appearance the work has made, in one place, linked to the outlet that ran it. Most of it is about Art Inside Out and the man who built it inside, which is the programme this organisation was founded around.

Every link here goes to the outlet that published it. Nothing on this page is hosted by us, edited by us, or embedded to play inside our site.

Writing about this?

We will talk to you, and we will tell you what we can and cannot prove. What we will not do is put a person we serve in front of a camera to make a story work. Anybody who appears does so because they asked to, in writing, and they can withdraw that at any point.
